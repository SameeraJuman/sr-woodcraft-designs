SR
Woodcraft & Designs

Born from a love of working with natural materials, SR Woodcraft & Designs started with a simple idea: that everyday objects should be made with care, creativity, and craft. Every piece that leaves our workshop is made by hand — measured, cut, shaped and finished with attention to every detail.

From intricate map puzzles that bring geography to life, to glowing acrylic light art that makes unforgettable gifts, we pour genuine passion into everything we create. Based in Guyana, we ship and deliver locally — and we love hearing the stories behind each custom order.
