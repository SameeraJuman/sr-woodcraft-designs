Got questions?

Common Questions

Helpful answers to your most important SR Woodcraft & Designs questions.

How do I place a custom order? +

Simply head to our Custom Orders page and fill in the quote request form — tell us what you have in mind, your preferred material, and any deadline. You can also reach us directly on WhatsApp and we'll guide you through the process.

How long does an order take? +

Turnaround time depends on the complexity of the piece. Simple carvings and stamps can be ready in 2–5 days, while detailed map puzzles or acrylic light art may take 1–2 weeks. We'll always give you an estimate when you enquire.

Can I send my own design? +

Absolutely! We love working from customer ideas. You can send sketches, images, or detailed descriptions via WhatsApp or the order form. If you have a digital file (PNG, SVG, PDF), even better — we'll work from it directly.

What materials do you use? +

We primarily work with quality natural wood and acrylic (perspex). The type of wood or acrylic used depends on the design — we'll recommend the best material for your piece when you get in touch.

Do you deliver outside of Guyana? +

Currently we primarily serve customers in Guyana with local pickup and delivery. For international orders, please contact us on WhatsApp to discuss shipping options and costs for your location.

How much does a custom piece cost? +

Pricing varies based on size, material, and complexity. We offer competitive rates and will always provide a clear quote before starting any work. Use the Custom Orders form or WhatsApp us for a free quote.
